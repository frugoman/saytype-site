How to dictate on a Mac offline (and keep your voice private)

Updated November 9, 2026

Two things people mean by "offline dictation": it should work with no internet (a plane, a bad connection), and the audio shouldn't be sent anywhere. They are related but not the same. Here is how to get both on a Mac.

Option 1: Apple's built-in Dictation

Apple has changed how Dictation works over the years. Older versions needed an "Enhanced Dictation" download to work offline. Newer macOS versions on Apple silicon process dictation on the device for many languages. Which applies to you depends on your macOS version and language, so check Apple's current Dictation support page for your version. A practical test: turn off Wi-Fi, open Notes and dictate. If text appears, it works offline for that language.

Limits to know about: you start it each time, it ends on its own after a pause, and you have little control over per-app behavior. See why Mac dictation stops by itself.

Option 2: A local speech model with SayType

SayType runs OpenAI's Whisper (through WhisperKit, using Core ML and the Neural Engine) entirely on your Mac. The default is Large v3 Turbo; there is also Parakeet v3 for 25 European languages. Setup:

  1. Install: brew install --cask frugoman/tap/saytype, or download the DMG from the releases page. Requires macOS 14 or later; Apple silicon recommended.
  2. Grant Microphone and Accessibility access when asked (System Settings → Privacy & Security).
  3. Let the speech model download once (about 630 MB, from Hugging Face). This is the only time speech features need internet. After that you can go offline.
  4. Click into any text field and talk. When you pause, the words are typed.

What stays on your Mac

Audio is kept in memory only long enough to transcribe it and is never saved or uploaded. History, snippets and vocabulary are stored in ~/Library/Application Support/SayType, and the history can be turned off. There are no accounts or analytics. The only network use is model downloads and Homebrew installs. The optional AI cleanup uses Apple's on-device model or a local server you point it at, such as Ollama or LM Studio. Because the code is open source on GitHub, you can check this yourself.

Offline checklist

  • Model downloaded while you still have internet.
  • Test with Wi-Fi off in a plain text field before you travel.
  • If you use AI cleanup, choose the on-device backend, not a remote server.
  • Transcribing audio files and recording meetings also work locally; meetings need the Screen & System Audio Recording permission.

FAQ

Can you dictate on a Mac without internet?

Yes. Newer macOS versions process Dictation on the device for many languages, and apps such as SayType run a speech model fully on your Mac. Test with Wi-Fi off in Notes to confirm for your setup.

Does SayType need internet?

Only to download the speech model once (about 630 MB) and to install or update through Homebrew. Dictating does not use the network.

Is my audio sent anywhere when I dictate with SayType?

No. Audio is transcribed on your Mac, kept in memory only long enough to do so, and never saved or uploaded.

What Macs does SayType support?

macOS 14 or later. Apple silicon is recommended for speed.
